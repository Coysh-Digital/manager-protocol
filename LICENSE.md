# Licence

MIT, deliberately, and for a different reason from the rest of Manager.

This package is the contract between the platform and the connector: the canonical strings that get
signed, the schemas that bound what may be collected, and the artifact encryption format. Restricting
it would work against the point of publishing it. Anyone should be able to read it, verify that a
signature or a payload is what it claims to be, or reimplement either side of the exchange.
